Goat Island Playground

The playground area features a lot to do for the whole family!

  • Large Playground for 5-12 yr olds
  • Treehouse Styled Adventure Structure
  • Ropes Climbing Course
  • Tower
  • Teeter-Totter
  • Swings
  • Slide

Adult Amentities Around the Playground Area:

  1. Workout Equipment in Outdoor Fitness Pavilion
  2. Corn Hole (bring your own bags)
  3. Ping Pong Table (bring your own paddles and ball)
  4. Picnic Shelters
  5. Fishing Pier
  6. Disc Golf
  7. Kayak and Canoe Launches
